# Beacon Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Beacon Survival SOS is designed as an offline emergency assistant. The app runs its AI model and retrieval system on the device.

## Data Collection

Beacon does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal data to the developer or to third-party analytics services.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or cloud inference APIs.

## On-Device Processing

Text prompts, selected photos, camera captures, language settings, model state, and emergency guidance are processed locally on the device.

Photos selected or captured for visual analysis are used for on-device inference. They are not uploaded by Beacon.

## Permissions

Beacon may request these permissions only when the related feature is used:

- Camera: to capture an image for local emergency visual analysis.
- Photo Library: to let the user choose an existing image for local visual analysis.
- Local storage: to keep app settings and offline resources on the device.

## Offline Knowledge

Beacon includes an offline knowledge bundle for emergency, survival, and public-safety retrieval. The App Store build uses a conservative source profile based on government, public-health, and public-safety materials with clearer reuse status.

## Medical and Safety Notice

Beacon provides emergency guidance support. It is not a replacement for licensed medical care, emergency services, official public-safety instructions, or professional rescue. When communications are available, contact local emergency services or qualified professionals.
